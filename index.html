About

I am an Associate Software Development Engineer at WhizHack Technologies, working at the intersection of cybersecurity and applied research. My day work is building production ML systems for ICS/OT security — anomaly detection, threat intelligence, and detection systems for industrial networks.

On the personal side, I'm currently working on vision-language-action (VLA) systems and studying reinforcement learning and the math underneath it.

I really like understanding new, interesting ideas — exploring them, rebuilding them from the ground up, and seeing where else they might fit or what I can do with them. I'm an IEEE-published researcher with strong mathematical foundations and engineering skills.
