About

I am an Associate Software Development Engineer at WhizHack Technologies, working at the intersection of cybersecurity and applied research — designing and building production-ready systems across intrusion detection, asset discovery, and threat intelligence for ICS/OT environments. My work spans the full arc from research to deployment — from low-level network configuration and kernel-level routing, building memory-safe protocol inspection modules, to shipping production-grade detection and discovery systems.

My current focus is on anomaly detection and inference in ICS/SCADA networks — systems where the signals are subtle, the stakes are high, and off-the-shelf solutions fall short. I am IEEE published, and have a research paper and patent in progress in this space.

Beyond the immediate work, I am drawn to fundamentally hard problems — the kind that demand deep thinking and careful engineering.
